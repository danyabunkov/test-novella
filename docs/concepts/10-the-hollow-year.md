# 10 - The Hollow Year

Genre: large folk-horror VN. Final engine target: Ren'Py.

Hook: In a village where the year never changes, you are the only one who remembers yesterday.

Prototype mechanic: loop-memory state machine. The player repeats days, keeps clues, and breaks the loop.

Production notes:
- Flagship project after the pipeline is proven.
- 6-8 character LoRAs, many backgrounds, 15-20 CG.
- Main risk is scope; fewer finished endings beat many unfinished ones.

Prototype done when:
- The loop repeats.
- Remembered clues persist.
- Collecting enough clues triggers an escape ending.
