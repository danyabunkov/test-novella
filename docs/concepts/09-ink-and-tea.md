# 09 - Ink & Tea

Genre: cozy VN-sim hybrid. Engine target: Godot, production may use Dialogic.

Hook: Open a tea house in a rainy city. Guests bring stories; you brew what they need.

Prototype mechanic: match tea choices to guest moods and grow friendship.

Production notes:
- Many guest LoRAs plus cafe backgrounds.
- Keep the sim loop light so it supports, not blocks, story.
- Dialogic is deferred until production; prototype uses simple data and UI.

Prototype done when:
- Guest mood is shown.
- Tea choice affects friendship.
- Day progression exists.
