# 04 - Last Train Home

Genre: casual runner/time-killer. Engine target: Godot.

Hook: One finger, the last train, an endless night.

Prototype mechanic: lane switching and jump/avoidance with score and instant restart.

Production notes:
- Neon night silhouette art is suitable for AI generation.
- Procedural obstacle generator must guarantee passable patterns.
- Better candidate for web/mobile retention than premium Steam.

Prototype done when:
- Player changes lane.
- Obstacles advance.
- Distance, near-miss, win/loss state exist.
